01 / How your customers book

From the first click to your chair

Four stops, and none of them needs your phone.

  1. Midday, 12:10

    Find the salon

    Your Instagram profile, your Google Business Profile or your website links to your booking page: yoursalon.kammzeit.com.

  2. Late evening, 23:47

    Book on their own

    Pick a service, a preferred staff member or anyone available, tap a free time, add name and email. No app and no customer account. The confirmation with a calendar entry arrives straight away.

  3. The day before

    Get a reminder

    24 hours before, an email arrives with all the details and a link to cancel. If something comes up, the slot is free again at once.

  4. Tuesday, 10:30

    In the chair

    The appointment is in your calendar, last time's colour formula is on the customer card. And you did not have to answer a single call for it.

02 / Set-up

Bookable online in three steps

  1. Create your salon

  3. Share the link

    In your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, on your website. From then on customers book on their own and get a confirmation like this one.

03 / Features

What Kammzeit takes care of

  • Calendar by day and week

    Appointments booked online and those you enter after a phone call sit side by side. If someone does not turn up, you mark it on the appointment.

  • Customer records with notes

    Every booking goes into your customer records, with its appointment history. Note the colour formula or preferred length right on the customer card.

  • Team with working hours

    Up to two time blocks a day for each staff member, so breaks are covered. You decide who does which service.

  • Reminder 24 hours before

    By email, with a link to cancel. So appointments get cancelled rather than forgotten.

  • Cancellation by link

    The slot can be booked again straight away, and you get an email.

  • Preferred staff member or anyone available

    Customers choose who they book with, or take whoever is free.

  • No app, no customer account

    The booking page runs in the browser, in English or German, you choose. Customers only enter their name and email address.

  • Your logo, your colour

    Logo, accent colour, opening hours and links to Instagram and more on your booking page. With Pro, a cover photo and a photo gallery too.

  • Rules you set

    How short-notice a booking can be, until when customers can cancel, and how far ahead they can book.

  • A queue for walk-ins

    With Pro: customers without an appointment join via QR code and see on their phone when it's their turn.

  • Logins for your team

    With Pro, every staff member logs in on their own and sees the calendar and customers. Plan and settings stay with you.

  • The dashboard as an app

    Install it on your phone or computer straight from the browser. On your first login, a short tour shows you around.

08 / Questions

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? Write to kontakt@kammzeit.de.

What does Kammzeit cost?

Nothing with one staff member, for as long as you like. For more staff, staff logins, the queue and no Kammzeit note there is Pro at €14.90 per month or €149 per year. No VAT is charged (German small business scheme, § 19 UStG). For business customers in other EU countries the reverse charge procedure may apply.

Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. The booking page runs in the browser on any phone or computer. Customers only enter their name and email address, the phone number is optional.

Is there a Kammzeit app?

For you, yes: install the dashboard on your phone or computer straight from the browser, no app store needed. It then opens like an app, with shortcuts to a new appointment, your customers and the queue. Your customers do not need an app.

Can my staff log in themselves?

With Pro, yes. You invite them from the Team page, and they log in with a link sent by email. Staff see the calendar and customers and keep their own working hours up to date. Plan, settings and export stay with you.

Is Kammzeit available in my language?

Kammzeit is available in English and German, from the website to the dashboard and the emails. Each salon chooses the language of its booking page.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in a data centre in Eygelshoven in the Netherlands, so inside the EU. For your customers' data there is a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR.

What helps against no-shows?

Every customer gets an email reminder 24 hours before, with a link to cancel. If someone still does not turn up, you mark the appointment as a no-show. Deposits at booking are planned.

Can I use my own domain?

Not yet, custom domains are planned. Your booking page runs at yoursalon.kammzeit.com, and you can already link to it from your website.
