01 / For your customers

Booking without an app or a phone call

Your booking page runs in the browser, on any phone. Customers need no account, just a name and an email address.

  • Your own booking page

    At yoursalon.kammzeit.com. Share the link in your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile or on your website.

  • Service, staff member, time

    Customers choose the service, then who with, or simply “anyone available”. Then a free time.

  • Only free times

    Customers only see times that fit the working hours and are not taken. Breaks and existing appointments are left out.

  • Confirmation with calendar entry

    Right after booking, a clear email arrives with your logo, all the details and a calendar file (.ics) for their own phone.

  • Reminder 24 hours before

    Automatically by email. So an appointment gets cancelled rather than forgotten.

  • Cancellation by link

    Up to the deadline you set. The slot can be booked again straight away, and you get an email.

  • No app, no customer account

    Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for. The phone number is optional.

  • Your logo, your colour

    Logo, accent colour, address, phone number and a few words about your salon. Plus your opening hours and links to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Google and WhatsApp.

  • In English or German

    You choose the language of your booking page. In German it runs at yoursalon.kammzeit.de, handy if most of your customers speak German.

  • Cover photo, gallery, design

    With Pro your booking page gets a cover photo, a photo gallery and a look of its own. More about Pro

02 / For you at the salon

Calendar, records and team in one place

In the dashboard at kammzeit.com. You log in with a link sent by email, there is no password.

Calendar by day and week

  • Online bookings and appointments you add yourself side by side, one column per staff member
  • Add a phone booking with one click on a free slot, the calendar suggests free times that fit
  • Reschedule appointments, mark no-shows and add an internal note that customers never see
  • An email to you for every online booking and every cancellation

Customer records with notes

  • Every booking creates a customer card or adds to an existing one
  • Notes such as colour formula, preferred length or favourite product stay with the customer
  • Appointment history: when someone came, what was done, who did not turn up
  • Export all your customer records as CSV

Team, services and breaks

  • Services with duration, price and a short description
  • Staff members with their own working hours, up to two time blocks a day, so breaks are included
  • You decide who does which service
  • One staff member on the Free plan, unlimited with Pro, each with their own login if you like

Your booking rules

  • Minimum notice: how short-notice an online booking can be
  • Cancellation deadline: until when customers can cancel on their own
  • Booking window: how far ahead customers can book

03 / Every day

Quick to set up, always at hand

  • Install it as an app

    Add the dashboard to your phone or computer straight from the browser, no app store needed. Shortcuts take you to a new appointment, your customers or the queue. The app keeps no customer data on the device.

  • A tour on your first login

    A short tour shows you where everything is. Restart it at any time with the question mark at the top of the dashboard or from the Help centre.

  • Every EU time zone

    From Lisbon to Helsinki: every time zone in the EU is available, plus Switzerland. Your booking page, calendar and emails all run on your salon's local time.

  • English and German

    Kammzeit is in English at kammzeit.com and in German at kammzeit.de, including the dashboard and emails. One account works on both.

  • Kammzeit news, if you want it

    New features and tips by email, only once you have confirmed. Unsubscribe at any time with one click or in your settings.

  • Help that actually helps

    The Help centre explains every feature step by step. And when you email a question, a real person answers.

04 / With Pro

For teams and busy salons

Pro costs €14.90 per month or €149 per year. It includes unlimited active staff, no Kammzeit note on your booking page and these three features.

A queue for walk-ins

  • Customers without an appointment scan a QR code in your salon and join the queue with their first name
  • On their phone they see live how many people are ahead of them and roughly how long it will take
  • If they leave an email address, they hear from you when you call them: you're up next
  • A screen in your salon shows the numbers, and you add people who ask at the counter yourself
  • You turn the queue on and off, and people can only join during your working hours

Logins for your team

  • You invite staff by email from the Team page, and they log in with a link just like you
  • Two roles: owner and staff member
  • Staff see the calendar, customers and notes, run the queue and keep their own working hours up to date
  • Plan, settings, services, team and export stay with you

Your booking page, in your style

  • A cover photo at the top of your booking page
  • A photo gallery with up to 12 pictures, for example hairstyles or nail designs
  • A choice of three fonts, rounded or square corners, light or dark and a welcome message of your own
  • Pictures are resized, and location data is removed from your photos

05 / Data and security

Your data stays yours

  • Servers in the EU

    In a data centre in Eygelshoven in the Netherlands. Bookings and customer records never leave the EU.

  • Data processing agreement

    For your customers' data there is a DPA under Art. 28 GDPR. No tracking, no ads.

  • Log in with an email link

    No password that could be guessed. With Pro everyone on your team logs in on their own and only sees what they need for their work.

  • Export at any time

    Customer records as CSV, all of your salon's data as a JSON file.

  • Delete your account yourself

    In the dashboard, without a phone call or a cancellation letter.

  • Open source

    The code is licensed under AGPL-3.0 and you can run it on your own server. Learn more

02 / Tax, payment, cancellation

The small print, in large letters

No VAT under § 19 UStG

Kammzeit is a small business under § 19 of the German VAT Act (UStG), so the invoice shows no VAT and the price here is the price you pay. For business customers in other EU countries the reverse charge procedure may apply.

Payment via Stripe

With the payment methods offered at checkout. The amount is due at the start of each month or year. Invoices arrive by email and are also in the customer portal.

Cancel in the dashboard

Under “Plan” via the customer portal, effective at the end of the paid month or year. Your salon then carries on with the Free plan: staff logins are paused, the queue is off, and the cover photo, design and gallery are hidden. Nothing is deleted.

The details are in the terms.

03 / Questions

Questions about pricing

Is there a trial for Pro?

No, that is what the Free plan is for, with no time limit. It includes everything except the Pro features: more staff, staff logins, the queue and the cover photo, design and gallery. So you can try Kammzeit with your salon at your own pace.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Pro stays available until the end of the paid period. After that, the limits of the Free plan apply again: one active staff member and the Kammzeit note on the booking page. Staff logins are paused, the queue is off, and the cover photo, design and gallery are hidden. Nothing is deleted: your data, your team's access and your pictures are kept and come straight back with Pro.

Does each staff member need their own Pro plan?

No. One Pro plan covers the whole salon, however many staff members get a login.

Are there costs per booking or per email?

No. There is no set-up fee and no charge per booking, per staff member or per email. Just the price of the plan.

Why is no VAT charged?

Kammzeit is run from Germany as a small business under § 19 UStG and does not charge VAT. If you are a business customer in another EU country, the reverse charge procedure may apply. Your accountant can tell you whether it does.

Are SMS reminders included?

Kammzeit does not send SMS. Reminders and confirmations go out by email, included in both plans at no extra cost.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes, electronically by email. You can also find all invoices in the customer portal, which you open in the dashboard under “Plan”.

Can I host Kammzeit myself?

Yes. The code is licensed under AGPL-3.0, and running it yourself costs nothing. You then look after the server, emails and updates yourself. More about open source
