Getting started

From creating your salon to your first test booking. Allow about ten minutes.

1. Create your salon

  1. Open the sign-up page, for example with the Start for free button on this website.
  2. Under Salon name, enter the name as it should appear on your booking page.
  3. Below it, Kammzeit suggests an address for your booking page, for example salon-example.kammzeit.com. You can use a-z, 0-9 and hyphens, 3 to 40 characters. Next to the field you can see whether the address is still available. It cannot be changed later.
  4. Under Your first name, enter your name. Customers book with you under this name. You can add more staff later.
  5. Under Email, enter the address you want to log in with. There is no password.
  6. Tick the box to confirm that you are signing up the salon as a business and accept the terms and the data processing agreement. The box for Kammzeit news below it is optional. Then click Create salon.

2. Log in with the link

You will see “Almost done”, and we send an email with your login link to the address you entered.

  1. Open the link on the device you want to use Kammzeit on. It is valid for 15 minutes and works only once.
  2. Click Log in now. You are now in your dashboard, in the Calendar. The first time, Kammi shows you around in a short tour.

No email? Check your spam folder. On the login page you can request a new link at any time. If you ticked Kammzeit news, a second email asks you to confirm. We only send news once you have confirmed.

Logging in again later

Open the login page, enter your Email, click Send login link, open the link in the email and click Log in now. You stay logged in on that device for 30 days. Log out is at the top right of the dashboard.

Your login belongs to exactly one salon. With Pro you can give your team their own logins, see Invite your staff.

3. What is already there

So you don't start from scratch, Kammzeit sets up a starting point when you sign up:

  • a staff member with your first name, working Tuesday to Friday 09:00-18:00 and Saturday 09:00-14:00,
  • two example services: a 30-minute haircut for €25.00 and a 60-minute wash, cut and blow-dry for €45.00.

The example services start out inactive. Nothing can be booked online until at least one service is active, so you can check everything in peace first.

At the top of the Calendar you will find a Getting started checklist with four steps. Each step is ticked off as soon as it is done, and the list disappears with your first online booking.

4. Set up

  1. Services: adjust names, durations and prices, add your own services and tick Active, bookable online. See Services.
  2. Team: open your staff member, check the working hours and the services they offer. Breaks go into the second block. See Team and working hours.
  3. Settings, Salon section: phone, address, website, the link to your salon's legal notice (imprint) and a few lines about your salon. All of this appears on your booking page.
  4. In the same section, check the Email for booking notifications. New bookings and cancellations are sent there. It is prefilled with your login address.
  5. Appearance section: your Logo, the Accent colour for buttons and highlights and the Booking page language (English or Deutsch). Your booking page and the emails to your customers use this language. See Customise your booking page.
  6. Booking rules section: how short-notice customers may book and cancel. The defaults are a minimum notice of 1 hour, cancellation up to the start of the appointment and a booking window of 60 days. See Booking rules.

Finish each page with Save. A confirmation then appears at the top.

5. Make a test booking

  1. Click Booking page at the top right of the dashboard. It opens in a new tab, exactly as your customers see it.
  2. Book an appointment with your own email address. You receive the confirmation with a calendar file and a cancellation link, and a notification about the new booking goes to your email for booking notifications.
  3. The appointment now appears in the Calendar. Cancel it with the link in the email, or open it in the calendar and click Cancel appointment.

6. Share your link

Now your customers just need to find the page: in your Instagram bio, your Google Business Profile, your WhatsApp status or as a button on your website. Share your booking link explains how.

Free and Pro: On the Free plan, one staff member can be active, and a small Kammzeit notice appears at the bottom of your booking page. Pro gives you unlimited staff, logins for your team, the queue for walk-ins, a cover image, design options and a photo gallery, and no notice. See Pro and cancelling.

Services

Services are the first thing customers choose on your booking page. Each one has a name, a duration and a price.

Add a new service

  1. Open Services in the menu, then click New service.
  2. Name: what your customers call it, for example “Women's cut, short hair”. Up to 80 characters.
  3. Description (optional, up to 300 characters): what is included, such as “including wash and blow-dry”. It appears on the booking page below the name.
  4. Duration in minutes: 5 to 480, in steps of 5.
  5. Price in euros: for example 25 or 25.50.
  6. Leave Active, bookable online ticked and click Save.

A new service is automatically assigned to all active staff. To change who offers which service, go to Team and open the staff member's Services section.

Choosing the right duration

The duration decides how long the staff member is busy. Your booking page only offers start times at which the whole service fits into the working hours without overlapping another appointment. Start times are offered every 15 minutes from the start of the working hours.

Include processing time and tidying up. If you need ten minutes after a colour to get the chair ready again, add them to the duration.

Order

In the list under Services, each row starts with two arrows. ↑ moves a service up, ↓ moves it down. Your booking page shows the services in this order, so put the one booked most often at the top.

Changing a service

Click the service in the list, change the values and click Save.

  • A new duration applies to new bookings. Appointments that are already booked keep their length.
  • A new price applies everywhere straight away: appointments and customer records always show the current price of the service. Customers pay in the salon, Kammzeit does not charge them anything.

Taking a service offline for a while

Open the service, untick Active, bookable online and click Save. The list then marks it as inactive. It can no longer be booked online or chosen for new appointments in the calendar. Existing appointments stay as they are. To bring it back, tick the box again.

Deleting a service

Open the service and click Delete service at the bottom, then Yes, delete. If the service already has appointments, Kammzeit does not delete it but deactivates it instead (the button then reads Yes, deactivate). That way appointments and the history in your customer records stay complete.

Team and working hours

A staff member can be booked online when they are active, have working hours and offer the service the customer has chosen.

Add or edit a staff member

  1. Open Team in the menu. Click a staff member in the list or New staff member.
  2. Name: this is how they appear on the booking page when customers choose who to book with. A first name is enough, up to 60 characters.
  3. Leave Active, bookable online ticked.
  4. In the Services section, tick what this person does. They can only be booked online for the ticked services.
  5. In the Working hours section, enter the times for each weekday and click Save.

For a new staff member, services and working hours are already filled in. That is only a suggestion, so adjust it before saving. In the team list, staff members without any services are flagged, because nobody can book them online.

Working hours and breaks

Each weekday has two blocks with a start and an end time. An empty day means a day off. The gap between the two blocks is a break that nobody can book into.

Example with a lunch break
DayBlock 1Block 2
Mondayemptyempty
Tuesday09:00 - 12:3013:30 - 18:00
Saturday09:00 - 14:00empty

Times go in steps of 5 minutes. Customers only see start times at which the chosen service fits completely into one block. In the example, a 60-minute service on Tuesday can start between 09:00 and 11:30 and between 13:30 and 17:00.

Who does which service

On the booking page, customers first choose the service, then who they want to book with: a particular staff member or Anyone available. Only staff members who have that service ticked are offered. With Anyone available, the customer sees the free times of all matching staff together.

More than one staff member

On the Free plan, one staff member can be active. If you add or activate a second one, the dashboard tells you that the Free plan allows only one active staff member and offers Upgrade to Pro. Inactive staff members don't count. With Pro you can have as many as you like.

With Pro, each staff member can also get their own dashboard login and then keep their working hours up to date under My hours. See Invite your staff.

Holidays and days off

Kammzeit does not yet have a feature for absences such as holidays or sick leave, it is planned. Here is how to manage in the meantime:

  • Longer absence: untick Active, bookable online for the staff member and save. Nothing can then be booked with them online. Appointments already in the calendar stay there: reschedule them to a colleague or cancel them one by one, see Reschedule and Cancellations. Tick the box again when they are back.
  • One weekday off for a while: clear the times for that day. This applies to every week until you enter the times again, so make a note of the old times first.
  • A single day or a few hours: add an appointment in the calendar for that time, for example with the customer name “Blocked”. Time that is taken cannot be booked online. Next time, find “Blocked” with the customer search instead of creating it again.

The earlier you adjust the times, the fewer appointments you have to move. How far ahead customers can book is set in the booking rules.

Booking rules

Three settings decide when customers can book and cancel online. They only apply to your booking page. In the calendar you can always add appointments yourself.

You will find them under Settings in the menu, in the Booking rules section. Click Save after making a change.

SettingOptionsDefault
Minimum noticeimmediately, or 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours or 1 day before1 hour before
Cancellation deadlineuntil the appointment starts, or until 1 hour, 2 hours, 1 day or 2 days beforeuntil the appointment starts
Booking window14, 30, 60, 90 or 180 days60 days

Minimum notice

The minimum notice stops a booking for ten minutes from now from catching you by surprise. With “2 hours before”, your booking page shows 11:00 as the first free time when it is 09:00. Customers who want to come sooner can call you, and you add the appointment to the calendar yourself.

Cancellation deadline

Until this point, customers can cancel themselves using the link in their email. If you set a deadline, it is shown on your booking page, in the confirmation and in the reminder. After the deadline, the cancellation page shows the phone number from your settings and asks the customer to call the salon.

Kammzeit cannot charge a cancellation fee, because customers pay nothing online.

Booking window

This is how far into the future your booking page shows free times. A short window (14 or 30 days) keeps you flexible if your hours change often. A long one (90 or 180 days) suits regulars who like to book their next appointment straight away.

Time zone

Also in the Booking rules section: the time zone of your salon. All times on your booking page, in emails and in the calendar are shown in this time zone. You can choose any EU country or Switzerland, so just pick the country your salon is in.

Share your booking link

Your booking page has its own address. The more places link to it, the more often customers book themselves instead of calling.

Find your address

Open Settings in the menu. Right at the top, under “Your booking page”, you will find the address, for example https://salon-example.kammzeit.com. Click Copy link to copy it. Or click Booking page at the top right of the dashboard and copy the address from your browser's address bar.

If your booking page is set to German, its address ends in .kammzeit.de. The .kammzeit.com address keeps working too, see Language and kammzeit.de.

Your own domain, such as bookings.your-salon.com, is not available on kammzeit.com yet. It is planned. If you host Kammzeit yourself, you can already use any domain.

Instagram

  1. Open your profile, tap Edit profile, then Links and Add external link.
  2. Paste the address, add a title such as “Book an appointment” and save.

In stories you can use the link sticker: create a story, tap the sticker icon, choose Link and paste the address. Handy for last-minute gaps such as “Free tomorrow at 14:00”.

Google Business Profile

Many people look up their salon on Google Search or Google Maps and tap the link in the profile.

  1. Sign in to Google with the account that manages your Business Profile and search for your salon's name.
  2. Choose Edit profile, then the section with your contact details.
  3. If you don't have a website of your own, enter your booking page under Website. If you do, put the booking page into the field for appointment links, if Google offers it to you.
  4. Save. Google sometimes reviews changes before they go live.

WhatsApp

  • Status: post a text with the link, for example “You can now book online: https://salon-example.kammzeit.com”. WhatsApp shows a preview of the page.
  • WhatsApp Business profile: in the settings, under the business tools, add your booking page as the website in your business profile.
  • WhatsApp Business quick replies: create a reply with the link, for example with the shortcut “/book”. Every booking request then takes just a tap.

A button on your website

Paste this HTML snippet where the button should appear and replace salon-example with your own address. Most website builders, such as WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or Jimdo, have a block called “HTML”, “Embed” or “Custom code” for this.

<a href="https://salon-example.kammzeit.com"
 style="display:inline-block;padding:14px 22px;background:#0a0a0a;color:#ffffff;font:700 16px/1.2 system-ui,sans-serif;text-decoration:none">
Book online
</a>

The colour of the button comes after background:. If a plain text link is enough, use just <a href="https://salon-example.kammzeit.com">Book online</a>.

More places

  • Your email signature and your Facebook page (the “Website” field).
  • Appointment cards, business cards and your shop window: the address is short enough to type. For the window, a QR code with the address works well. Any QR code generator can make one. For walk-ins, Pro comes with a separate QR code for joining the queue, see Queue.
  • Your voicemail greeting: “You can also book online at …”

Instagram, Google and WhatsApp rename their menus from time to time. If in doubt, look for “Link” or “Website”.

Add appointments

Online bookings appear in the calendar by themselves. Appointments made on the phone or at the front desk you add yourself, and the time is then taken online too.

Reading the calendar

The Calendar is the start page of your dashboard. At the top you see the appointments today and the next appointment. Below that, switch between Day and Week, move with Previous day and Next day (in the week view: Previous week and Next week), go back with Today or pick a date and click Show.

In the day view, each staff member has a column in their own colour. White areas are working hours, hatched areas are time off or breaks, and a coral line marks the current time. Cancelled appointments have a grey outline and the name crossed out. No-shows have a coral outline.

Add an appointment

  1. In the day view, click a free spot in the staff member's column. Date, staff member and time are then already filled in. Or click New appointment at the top right.
  2. Choose the Service and the Staff member and check the Date.
  3. Pick a Time. Kammzeit suggests the free times of that staff member as buttons, but you can type any time in steps of 5 minutes, even outside working hours, for example before opening.
  4. Under Customer: type a name, email or phone number into Search existing customers and select the match. Or choose New customer and enter the name and, if you have them, phone number and email.
  5. If you like, add a Note on this appointment, such as “bringing a photo”. Only your salon can see it.
  6. If the customer has an email address, you can tick Send confirmation with cancellation link by email.
  7. Click Save.

If the email address or phone number is already in your customer records, Kammzeit adds the appointment to that customer instead of creating a second one.

No double bookings

If the staff member already has an appointment at the chosen time, Kammzeit does not save and tells you when they are busy, for example that Lisa already has an appointment from 10:00 to 10:30. The other way round, every appointment you add is immediately taken on your booking page too.

Reminders for appointments you add

If the customer has an email address, they get a reminder 24 hours before the appointment, even if you didn't send a confirmation. If you add an appointment that is less than 24 hours away, there is no reminder. Customers without an email address don't receive any emails. Kammzeit does not send text messages.

Tip: Ask for the email address on the phone. With a reminder and a cancellation link, customers forget their appointment less often, and anyone who can't make it cancels with one click.

Appointment details and notes

Clicking an appointment shows the time, service, price, staff member and customer with email and phone (on a mobile, tap the number to call). The source tells you whether the appointment was booked online or added in the dashboard.

Two kinds of notes appear there: the customer note from the customer card, which applies to every appointment of that person, and the note on this appointment, which you can change and keep with Save note.

Rescheduling

  1. Open the appointment and click Reschedule.
  2. Choose the new staff member, date and time. Again, Kammzeit suggests free times.
  3. If the customer has an email address, you can let them know about the new time by email. If you have already agreed on it, untick the box.
  4. Click Reschedule.

Customers cannot reschedule themselves. They cancel with the link in their email and book again. More in Cancellations and no-shows.

Customer records

Like the card box at your front desk, only searchable: contact details, notes and all appointments of a customer in one place.

How customers get into your records

Anyone who books online is added automatically. If the same email address books again, the appointment goes onto the same customer card. Regulars who don't book online you add yourself: under Customers in the menu with New customer, or directly while adding an appointment.

There is no import from other software yet, it is planned.

Searching

The search field under Customers finds people by name, email or phone number as you type. The list shows contact details, the number of visits, the last visit and the next appointment.

The customer card

Click a name to open the customer card: contact details, customer since, visits, next appointment and revenue so far. Below that you find all upcoming and past appointments with service, staff member, price and status.

  • Visits counts completed appointments. Cancelled appointments and no-shows don't count.
  • Revenue so far is the sum of the current service prices, for orientation only. Kammzeit doesn't know what was actually paid.
  • New appointment at the top opens the appointment form with this customer already selected.

Notes

Open Edit contact details and notes. The Notes field is for anything you want to know at the next visit: the colour formula (“7/1 + 20 vol.”), the favourite cut, special wishes. Up to 2000 characters, then click Save. Notes are only visible to your salon in the dashboard, never to the customer.

The note on the customer card applies to the person and appears with each of their appointments. The note on an appointment applies to that one appointment only.

Sensitive information: allergies, skin conditions and other health data are special categories of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR. Only record them with the person's explicit consent, and in a way that lets you prove that consent. Kammzeit protects these notes like all other data but does not encrypt them separately. More in the data processing agreement and under Data and security.

CSV export

Under Customers, below the list, click Download all customers as a CSV file. You get a file with name, email, phone, notes, the date the customer was added and the number of visits. It is saved in UTF-8 with semicolons between the columns, so accents and special characters come out right. If everything ends up in one column in Excel, import it via Data › From Text/CSV and choose the semicolon as the delimiter. LibreOffice and Numbers ask for the delimiter when opening.

The file contains your customers' personal data. Don't leave it on a computer that others use, and delete it when you no longer need it. Only the salon owner can export and delete customers, not a staff login.

Changing or deleting a customer

Change contact details with Edit contact details and notes. To delete a customer, click Delete customer at the bottom of the customer card, then Delete permanently. This removes the contact details, notes and all appointments of this customer, including upcoming ones. The customer is not notified. This is how you fulfil a customer's request to have their data erased, for example.

Cancellations and no-shows

A reminder the day before, a cancellation link in every email and a note of who didn't turn up. What runs by itself and what you do.

Reminder 24 hours before

Every booked appointment gets a reminder email 24 hours before it starts, with the time, service, staff member, your address, a calendar file and the cancellation link. There is nothing to set up.

  • If someone books less than 24 hours ahead, the confirmation takes the place of the reminder.
  • Customers without an email address don't receive any emails. There are no text messages.

When a customer cancels

  1. The confirmation and the reminder ask the customer to cancel if they can't make it, so the time becomes free for others, and contain the cancellation link.
  2. The customer opens the link and clicks Cancel appointment. Opening the link alone doesn't cancel anything, so link previews in email apps can't cancel an appointment by accident.
  3. The customer receives a cancellation confirmation with a link to book again. A notification of the cancellation goes to your email for booking notifications.
  4. In the calendar, the appointment is shown as cancelled. The time is free online again straight away.

You decide until when this is possible under Settings › Booking rules › Cancellation deadline. After the deadline, the cancellation page shows your phone number. See Booking rules.

When you cancel

  1. Click the appointment in the calendar.
  2. Click Cancel appointment. If the customer has an email address and the appointment is still to come, the box to notify the customer by email is ticked. They then get an email with a link to book again and your phone number. If you have already spoken, untick it.
  3. Click Yes, cancel. The time can be booked online again straight away.

If only the time changes, you don't need to cancel: reschedule the appointment instead.

No-shows

Once the appointment has started, it shows a No-show box with the button Mark as no-show. The calendar then shows the appointment as a no-show, and so does the customer card. Next time the customer calls, you can see who has missed an appointment before.

The mark cannot be undone. If the customer is just running late, wait before you click.

Preventing no-shows

  • Reminders only work with an email address. Ask for it when someone books by phone.
  • Cancelling by link is easier for customers than calling. With a deadline such as “until 2 hours before”, the time usually becomes free early enough to fill it.
  • Kammzeit cannot take deposits or charge cancellation fees.

Everything else is the same on both plans: booking page, calendar, customer records, reminders, app and export. All prices are final prices. No VAT is charged (German small business scheme, § 19 UStG). For business customers in other EU countries the reverse charge procedure may apply.

Switching to Pro

  1. Open Plan in the menu.
  2. Choose Pro monthly or Pro yearly.
  3. You are taken to Stripe, our payment provider. Enter your payment details and complete the purchase.
  4. Back in the dashboard, a message tells you that your Pro plan will be active as soon as the payment is confirmed. That usually takes just a few seconds. The Plan page then shows Pro as your plan.

Payment method, invoices, cancelling

  1. Open Plan in the menu and click Manage / cancel plan.
  2. In Stripe's customer portal you can change your payment method, download invoices or cancel.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for, that is the current month or year. Pro stays active until then. After that, your account continues on the Free plan. Your data stays, and the limits of the Free plan apply again. Only the salon owner can manage the plan.

What happens when Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Some things just rest until you upgrade again:

A queue for walk-in customers

Walk-ins scan a QR code, enter their first name and see on their phone when it's their turn. You call them with one click. The queue comes with Pro.

Turn it on

  1. Open Queue in the menu and click Turn queue on. The page now shows On.
  2. If you like, add a few words under Note for customers. They appear above the form, for example “Please stay close by, we'll call you.” Up to 300 characters, then click Save note.

Customers can only join while someone is working according to your working hours. Outside those hours they see a notice that you are closed. While the queue is on and you are open, your booking page also shows “Dropping in without an appointment? Join the queue”.

Only the salon owner can turn the queue on or off and change the note. Staff members with their own login work the list. On the Free plan, the menu item explains what the queue can do and offers Upgrade to Pro.

Print the QR code

  1. On the Queue page, in the box For your customers, click Print QR code.
  2. A sheet opens with your salon's name, “No appointment? Join the queue online.”, the QR code, three short steps and the address to type in. Click Print.
  3. Put the sheet on the door, in the window or at the front desk.

The address for joining is also shown on the Queue page, for example salon-example.kammzeit.com/warteschlange. You can share it just like your booking link.

Screen for your salon

Screen for your salon opens a page in a new tab for a TV or tablet in your salon: “Now serving” in large type with the numbers that have been called, the waiting numbers below and a QR code with “Join here”. Full screen hides everything else. The screen updates itself every few seconds and only shows numbers, never names.

What your customers see

  1. They scan the QR code, enter their First name, an email address and the service if they like, and tap Join the queue. They don't need an account.
  2. Their phone shows their number, how many people are ahead of them and the estimated wait. The page updates by itself.
  3. Anyone who decides not to wait taps I'm not coming after all.

Kammzeit estimates the wait from the duration of the chosen services (20 minutes per person if none is chosen), divided by the number of staff working today.

Call, Serving, Done, Not here

The Queue page lists everyone waiting today with their number, waiting time and chosen service. The list refreshes every ten seconds.

  • Call: it's this person's turn. Their phone says it's their turn and asks them to come to the reception, and the screen in your salon shows their number.
  • Serving: the person is in the chair. You can also use it straight away without calling first.
  • Done: finished, the person leaves the list.
  • Not here: called but didn't turn up. Their phone asks them to check in at the reception.
  • Remove: takes someone off the list without further ado.

If someone asks at the counter, add them on the right under Add a person with their name and click Add to queue.

An email when it's their turn

If someone gave an email address when joining, they get an email “It's your turn” with their number as soon as you click Call. So they can pop out for a coffee in the meantime. In the list, these people are marked “gets an email”. The email is sent in the language of your booking page.

Data

We only need the first name and email address for today's queue. The next day, Kammzeit deletes the previous day's entries. So that customers find their place again after reloading, their phone remembers it in a cookie (kz_queue) for up to 12 hours. More under Data and security.

03 / Self-hosting

On your own server in five steps

  1. Server and domain. A Linux server with systemd, ports 80 and 443 open, a domain with an A or AAAA record pointing to the server and an SMTP account for emails.
  2. Adapt the legal texts and build. Replace the imprint and privacy policy in web/legal.html with your own, then build with Go. The result is a single file that contains everything.
  3. Install. Create a dedicated system user, copy the file to /usr/local/bin and add the systemd unit from deploy/. /etc/kammzeit.env holds KAMMZEIT_SINGLE_TENANT=1, your address, the path to the database and the SMTP details.
  4. Put Caddy in front. Three lines in the Caddyfile. Caddy gets the certificate by itself and redirects to HTTPS.
  5. Set up and back up. Open /registrieren, create your salon and log in with the link from the email. Add a daily backup of the database with sqlite3 .backup using a systemd timer.
# Step 2: get the code and build
git clone https://github.com/luxend0/kammzeit.git
cd kammzeit
CGO_ENABLED=0 go build -trimpath -ldflags="-s -w" -o kammzeit .

# Step 4: /etc/caddy/Caddyfile
bookings.your-salon.com {
	reverse_proxy 127.0.0.1:8080
}

Where the data is stored

Kammzeit runs on InstantNode's own servers in the data centre of Skylink Data Centers B.V. in Eygelshoven (Kerkrade), Netherlands. The database, backups and email sending run on our own systems in the EU. Administration takes place from the EU or the EEA.

Tube-Hosting filters incoming traffic with PletX technology to protect against overload attacks. Only connection data such as IP addresses is processed for this, never content. Stripe only processes the billing data for your Pro plan and never data about your customers.

Who has access

Only we as the operator have access to the servers, database and backups. Other people only if they have first been bound to confidentiality in writing. We only look at content if you ask us to, if it is technically necessary for operation, backups or fixing a fault, if there is a specific suspicion of abuse or a security incident, or if the law requires it.

We don't pass data on to third parties and we don't sell anything. We don't keep access logs. The server's error logs are deleted after 14 days.

Separation between salons

All salons share one database, and the separation happens in the application: every record belongs to exactly one salon, and every query in the dashboard is limited to the salon that is logged in. A salon account can only see its own data. Every booking page has its own subdomain. The program runs as a separate, restricted system user that can only write to its own data directory.

Roles in your salon

With Pro, the salon owner can give staff their own logins. Every login has its own email address and logs in by itself with a link. There is no need to share one login.

  • Staff see the calendar, customers with their notes and the queue, and keep their own working hours up to date.
  • They have no access to the plan, settings, services and team, the data export, the CSV export of customers, deleting customers or deleting the account. The server checks this on every request, not just the menu.
  • When the owner removes an access, all sessions of that login end immediately. If a staff member is inactive or Pro ends, the login can no longer be used.

How this works in the dashboard is described in Invite your staff.

Login and cookies

  • No password: to log in, we send a link to your email address. It is valid for 15 minutes and works only once.
  • We store login links and session IDs only as SHA-256 hashes, never in plain text.
  • The session cookie kz_session cannot be read by scripts (HttpOnly), is only sent over encrypted connections (Secure), is only valid for the site you logged in on, expires after 30 days and becomes invalid as soon as you log out.
  • The cookie kz_queue is set by the queue on the phone of a customer who joins. It only remembers their place for today, is only valid for that salon's queue, can't be read by scripts and expires after 12 hours.
  • Kammzeit sets no other cookies, none for statistics or advertising either.
  • Changes in the dashboard are only accepted from forms whose origin is checked. A Content Security Policy prevents scripts from other sources from being loaded. Login, booking, the queue and uploads are rate-limited against mass requests.

Your part: anyone who can open your email inbox can log in. Protect it with a strong password and ideally two-factor authentication, log out on shared devices and tell us straight away if something seems wrong.

Encryption

All connections to the website, the booking pages and the dashboard are encrypted with TLS. We administer the servers via SSH. Kammzeit does not additionally encrypt notes in your customer records. Health data such as allergies should only go into the records with explicit consent, see Customer records.

App and device storage

The dashboard can be installed as an app, see Install the app. The app's service worker only stores fixed files on the device, such as the stylesheet, font, images and the offline page. Pages with appointments, customers, login or session data are always loaded from the network and never kept in the device's storage. So no customer data is left behind on a front desk computer or a shared tablet.

In the browser's storage, Kammzeit only remembers two small things: that you closed the install notice and that you have already seen the dashboard tour. Neither contains personal content.

Uploaded images

We only accept the logo, cover image and gallery photos as PNG, JPEG or WebP, no SVG, because SVG files can contain scripts. Every image is checked, resized and saved anew. This removes all metadata: camera details, the time it was taken and the GPS location from the EXIF data. The images are stored in the database, belong to exactly one salon like all other data, are backed up with it and removed when the account is deleted. They are publicly visible on your booking page, so only upload what should be shown there.

Backups and deletion

The database is backed up regularly on InstantNode systems in the EU, and each backup is kept for 30 days at most. If you delete your account, we immediately remove all of your salon's data: customer records, appointments, staff and their logins, services, images, login links and sessions. It disappears from the backups no later than 30 days afterwards. Expired sessions are deleted automatically, queue entries the following day.

  • Remove your staff's access at any time: Invite your staff.
  • Delete your account for good, at any time, in the dashboard.

01 / General

What Kammzeit is

What is Kammzeit?

Online booking for hairdressers, barbershops, beauty salons and nail salons. Your customers book on your own booking page, such as your-salon.kammzeit.com, and you see everything in the calendar in your dashboard, together with the appointments you add yourself.

Which salons is Kammzeit for?

For salons with set services and working hours, from one-person studios to teams, with or without walk-ins. It is not the right fit if you need to schedule rooms, treatment cabins or equipment, or want to run several locations from one account. Kammzeit can't do that. Each location would need its own account with its own email address.

Do I or my customers need an app?

Your customers don't: they book in the browser, without an app and without creating an account. Your dashboard runs in the browser too, and you can also install it like an app on your phone, tablet or computer, without an app store. Install the app

Which languages does Kammzeit support?

English and German, more are planned. One account works on both sites: the dashboard is in English on kammzeit.com and in German on kammzeit.de. For your booking page, you choose the language under Booking page language in Settings. Your customers then book and receive their emails in that language. Language and kammzeit.de

Can I try Kammzeit first?

Yes. The demo salon shows the booking page as your customers see it. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no payment details: create a salon, look around the dashboard and make a test booking on your own booking page. A short tour shows you around the first time you log in. Getting started

02 / Prices

What it costs

How much does Kammzeit cost?

Free gives you one active staff member, with a small Kammzeit notice on your booking page. Pro costs €14.90 per month or €149 per year: unlimited staff, logins for your team, a queue for walk-ins, cover image, design and photo gallery, and no notice. Customers pay nothing to book. Free and Pro compared

Is VAT added?

No. All prices are final prices. No VAT is charged (German small business scheme, § 19 UStG). For business customers in other EU countries the reverse charge procedure may apply. If in doubt about how to record this in your own accounts, ask your tax adviser.

Do I get invoices?

Yes. You can download every invoice for Pro in Stripe's customer portal: Plan in the menu, then Manage / cancel plan.

Are there fees per booking or for emails?

No. No fee per booking, no charge for confirmations and reminders, no setup fee.

Is the Free plan limited in time?

No, it runs indefinitely and without payment details. You can end it at any time by deleting your account. Under our terms, we can also end it with four weeks' notice.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the dashboard under Plan, Manage / cancel plan. Pro runs until the end of the period you have paid for, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your data stays, staff logins are paused, and the cover image, design and gallery are hidden. Guide

Can I show my prices in another currency?

Not at the moment. Service prices are entered and shown in euros, including on your booking page.

03 / In your salon

Team, walk-ins, design

Do my staff get their own logins?

Yes, with Pro. You invite them under Team with their email address, and they log in with a link just like you, without a password. Invite your staff

What can staff with their own login see?

The calendar, customers with their notes and the queue. They add, reschedule and cancel appointments and keep their own working hours up to date under My hours. The plan, settings, services, team, exports and deleting customers stay with you.

Is there a queue for walk-in customers?

Yes, with Pro. Customers scan a QR code in your salon, enter their first name and see on their phone when it's their turn. Anyone who leaves an email address gets an email when they are called. A screen in your salon can show the numbers. Queue

Can I add my logo and style the booking page?

Yes. Logo, accent colour, social links and opening hours are available to everyone. Pro adds a cover image, font, rounded corners, a dark design, a welcome message and a photo gallery with up to 12 photos. Customise your booking page

04 / Customers

For your customers

Do my customers need an account?

No. When booking, they enter their name and email address. The phone number is optional.

Which emails do my customers receive?

A confirmation with a calendar file and a cancellation link, a reminder 24 hours before the appointment, an email with the new time if you reschedule and tick the box, and a cancellation confirmation if they cancel. All in the language of your booking page. You receive an email for every new booking and every cancellation.

Can customers cancel or reschedule themselves?

They can cancel using the link in their email, up to the deadline you set. To reschedule, they cancel and book again. You can reschedule any appointment in the dashboard. More about cancellations

Are there text message reminders?

No, Kammzeit only sends reminders by email.

Can customers pay or leave a deposit online?

No, customers pay in the salon. Kammzeit does not offer online payment or deposits.

Can I bring my customers over from other software?

Not yet, importing from a CSV file is planned. Until then, you add regulars to your customer records one by one, or they are added automatically as soon as they book online.

05 / Data

Your data

Where is the data stored?

On our own servers in a data centre in Eygelshoven in the Netherlands, so in the EU. No data is transferred to countries outside the EU. Data and security

Can I use Kammzeit in line with the GDPR?

Kammzeit is built for it: servers in the EU, no tracking, a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR, and tools to export, correct and delete customer data when someone asks. As the salon, you remain the controller for your customers' data, for example for informing them in your own privacy notice.

Is there a data processing agreement?

Yes. You accept it when you sign up and can read it at any time under DPA. The English version is a translation; the German version is legally binding.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes, at any time: all data as a JSON file in Settings, and your customer records as a CSV file for spreadsheets as well. Guide

What happens to the data if I stop?

If you delete your account, the data is removed from the live system immediately and from the backups no later than 30 days afterwards. If you only cancel Pro, everything stays on the Free plan.

Do you send newsletters or advertising?

Only if you subscribe to Kammzeit news: it is optional, confirmed by email and you can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email or in Settings. It is only about Kammzeit, never ads for anyone else. Emails about your account, such as login links and bookings, reach you regardless. Kammzeit news

Are photos published with location data?

No. When you upload a logo, cover image or gallery photos, Kammzeit removes all metadata, including camera details and the GPS location. More on this

May I note allergies or intolerances?

That is health data under Art. 9 GDPR. Only note it with the person's explicit consent. Kammzeit does not additionally encrypt notes. More on this

06 / Technical

Technology and self-hosting

Can I use my own domain?

Not on kammzeit.com yet, it is planned. Until then your booking page has an address like your-salon.kammzeit.com, or your-salon.kammzeit.de in German. If you host Kammzeit yourself, you can use any domain.

Does it connect to Google Calendar?

Not yet, syncing with Google Calendar is planned. With every booking, your customers get a calendar file for their phone.

Does Kammzeit work without internet?

No. Appointments and customers are not stored on the device, so without a connection the app only shows a notice and reloads as soon as you are back online.

Can I host Kammzeit myself?

Yes. The code is published under the AGPL-3.0. You need a Linux server, a domain and an SMTP account. Self-hosted, all Pro features are included. Open source and self-hosting
