01 / Example price list

Your services, the way you plan them

This is how a hair salon might set up its services in Kammzeit. You choose the names, durations and prices, and your customers see them exactly like this on your booking page. The prices are only examples.

02 / A day on the grid

A break is a break

On Tuesdays, Anna works from 9:00 to 13:00 and from 14:00 to 18:00. In Kammzeit, that's two blocks on the same day. The booking page offers start times every 15 minutes, but only those where the whole appointment fits into one block.

03 / Salon life

Situations you know

Only Anna does balayage.

You decide who offers which service. When someone books balayage, they can only choose Anna or “Anyone available”, and they only see Anna's free times. Apprentices can be left out of services they don't do yet.

Your apprentice is at college on Tuesdays.

Working hours are set per weekday. If you enter nothing for your apprentice on Tuesdays, they can't be booked that day. They only work until 14:00 on Mondays? You set that for the weekday, too.

What colour did she have last time?

Every customer has a customer card with your notes and all their appointments. If it says “7/1 + 7/0, 6%, 1:1, 35 min”, you don't have to guess at the next root touch-up.

An allergy to hair dye or a scalp condition is health data, a special category of personal data under the GDPR. Only note something like this if the customer explicitly agrees, and keep it as brief as possible.

Your regular still phones.

You add phone bookings to the calendar yourself. If the time is already taken, Kammzeit tells you. If you like, the customer gets a confirmation email with a link to cancel.

Saturday, 9:00, and the chair stays empty.

Customers with an email address get a reminder 24 hours before, with a link to cancel. Anyone who can't make it cancels there, and the time can be booked again straight away. You decide how late customers can cancel online, from “until the appointment starts” to “two days before”.

Someone books for ten minutes from now.

With the minimum notice, you decide how close to the appointment customers can still book online, from “immediately” to one day before. That way nothing lands in your calendar while you're in the middle of a colour.

05 / To be honest

What Kammzeit can't do for hairdressers yet

Using processing time

An appointment blocks the person for its whole duration. You can't fit a men's cut into the processing time of a colour, neither online nor in the calendar.

Several services in one booking

Customers choose one service per booking. Set up combinations like “colour and cut” as a service of their own.

Deposit when booking

Nobody pays anything online. Reminders, the cancellation deadline and the no-show mark on the customer card help against missed appointments.

Text messages

Confirmations and reminders are sent by email. Kammzeit doesn't send text messages (SMS).

02 / A Saturday

Booked online or walked in: all in one place

03 / The queue

Walk-ins join the queue themselves

Customers without an appointment don't have to ask at the counter how long it will take. They scan the QR code, enter their first name and see on their phone how many people are ahead of them. The queue is part of the Pro plan. Adding walk-ins straight to the calendar works on every plan.

Put up the QR code, done. You print the QR code from your dashboard. Customers don't need an app or an account, just their first name; the email address is optional. While the queue is open, your booking page also shows “Join the queue”.

One click calls the next customer. You call the next number, and anyone who left an email address gets an email saying it's nearly their turn. A screen or tablet in the shop shows the numbers and the QR code for joining. If someone asks at the counter, you add them yourself.

On when you want it. You switch the queue on and off. Outside your working hours it closes automatically, and with their own login, every barber can call customers.

04 / Shop life

Situations you know

The shop is full, and someone books for five minutes from now.

With the minimum notice, you set how close to the appointment customers can still book online: immediately, 30 minutes, one hour or longer before. If you like spontaneous customers, leave it at “immediately”.

Only one of you does hot towel shaves.

You decide who offers which service. Anyone booking a hot towel shave can only choose Murat or “Anyone available”, and only sees Murat's free times.

The same fade every three weeks.

His customer card has a note: “Sides 0.5 to 2, top 4 cm with scissors, square neckline”. So everyone on the team cuts it the same way, even when his usual barber is off.

Saturday's 9:00 customer doesn't turn up.

Customers with an email address get a reminder 24 hours before, with a link to cancel, so they're more likely to cancel than forget. If someone still doesn't come, you mark the appointment as a no-show. Next time, the customer card shows you who you can count on.

Fridays from noon, but until late.

Every barber has their own working hours for each weekday, with up to two blocks per day. Someone who works Fridays from 12:00 to 15:00 and from 16:00 to 21:00 can be booked exactly then.

The link belongs in your Instagram bio.

Your booking page has its own address, such as yourshop.kammzeit.com. Customers don't need an app or an account, just their name and email address.

01 / Why Kammzeit

What Kammzeit cares about

Simple

One booking page, one calendar, one set of customer records. Your customers don't need an app or an account, and you don't need a password: you log in with a link sent by email.

Fairly priced

Free for one person, with no time limit. For the whole team, €14.90 a month or €149 a year, with no setup fee and cancellable at the end of the paid period. No VAT is charged (German small business scheme, § 19 UStG). Details on the pricing page.

Data in the EU

Appointments and customer data are stored on our own servers in the Netherlands and don't leave the EU. No tracking, and no data passed on for advertising. There is a data processing agreement for your customers' data.

No lock-in

You can take your data with you at any time: the customer records as CSV, everything together as JSON. And because the code is open, you can also run Kammzeit on a server of your own.
