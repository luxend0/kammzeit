Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

{{template "en-legal-note" .}}

1. Controller

Ben Oliver Mallow, Sickinger Straße 7, 55758 Sien, Germany, email kontakt@kammzeit.de. Further details can be found in our imprint.

2. Two roles

We are the controller for the websites kammzeit.de and kammzeit.com and for the salons' accounts. For bookings made on a salon's page (e.g. salonname.kammzeit.de or salonname.kammzeit.com), the salon is the controller. We process this data only on the salon's behalf and on its instructions (processing by a processor under Art. 28 GDPR, see our data processing agreement). Questions about a booking are best sent directly to the salon.

3. Hosting

Kammzeit runs on InstantNode's own servers in a data centre in Eygelshoven (Netherlands), i.e. within the EU. The data centre is operated by Skylink Data Centers B.V. Traffic to our servers is filtered against overload attacks by Tube-Hosting (Ferdinand Zink, Germany) using PletX technology. Only connection data such as IP addresses is processed for this, not content, because the connections are encrypted. Both service providers are based in the EU and act as processors.

Booking data and data from the customer records do not leave the EU. The only exception relating to third countries is payment for the Pro plan via Stripe (section 8), which only concerns salons.

4. Visiting the website

When you visit the website, the server processes your IP address and the requested address because this is technically necessary to deliver the page (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not keep access logs. The server's error logs may contain email addresses (for example if an email could not be delivered) and are deleted after 14 days.

To protect against misuse, we limit how often certain actions can be carried out within a short time, such as logins, sign-ups and bookings. For this purpose, the server briefly keeps the IP address and, where applicable, the email address entered, together with the time, in memory. This information is not written to disk, not analysed and is discarded at the latest when the service restarts. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Kammzeit is available in German at kammzeit.de and in English at kammzeit.com. If your browser states German as its preferred language, we redirect you from the home page of kammzeit.com to kammzeit.de. For this, the server only reads the language setting that your browser sends with every request anyway (Accept-Language). We do not store it and do not set a cookie for it. If you come from one of our own pages or open kammzeit.com/?lang=en, there is no redirect. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing the website in your language (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

There are no tracking or analytics tools, no advertising and no third-party content or scripts on our pages.

5. Cookies

We set exactly one cookie, and only when a salon logs in to the dashboard:

  • kz_session: keeps you logged in. It contains a random identifier, is only valid for the domain on which you log in (kammzeit.de or kammzeit.com, not for the booking pages), cannot be read by scripts (HttpOnly), is only sent over encrypted connections (Secure) and expires after 30 days. When you log out, we delete it immediately.

The cookie is strictly necessary for us to provide the service you have expressly requested (the dashboard). No consent is required for it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). The associated processing serves the performance of the contract with the salon (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Anyone who only uses Kammzeit to book an appointment does not receive a cookie.

6. Registration and salon account

When you create a salon account, we process the email address used for logging in, the name of the salon and the desired address of the booking page. In the dashboard you can add further information, such as the salon's address, phone number, website and imprint, services, and the names and working hours of staff. Information intended for the booking page is publicly visible there. We also store when the account was created and when you last logged in.

The purpose is to provide Kammzeit in accordance with our terms and conditions; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Registration is not possible without an email address, because logging in only works via email. We also use the email address to send you notices relating to the contract, for example about changes to the terms and conditions, maintenance or payments. We do not send advertising.

7. Logging in with an email link

Kammzeit has no passwords. To log in, we send a link to the email address on file. The link is valid for 15 minutes and can only be used once. In the database we store it only as a cryptographic hash, and the same applies to the session identifier. We delete expired sessions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Pro plan and payment via Stripe

You pay for the Pro plan via Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland ("Stripe"). To pay, you are redirected to a Stripe page. We do not embed any Stripe scripts on kammzeit.de or kammzeit.com.

Stripe processes the data required for payment, in particular name, email address, billing address, payment details (e.g. card details or IBAN), amount and time, as well as technical data such as the IP address for fraud prevention. We do not receive full card details or the IBAN. Stripe tells us whether and when payment was made and assigns identifiers for the customer and the subscription, which we store with the salon account. In Stripe's customer portal you can change the payment method and billing details, download invoices and cancel the plan.

The purpose is to handle the subscription. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and, for the retention of invoices, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with § 147 AO (German Fiscal Code) and § 257 HGB (German Commercial Code). Stripe is an independent controller for payment processing, fraud prevention and compliance with its own legal obligations. Stripe's privacy policy applies.

Stripe may transfer data to Stripe, Inc. in the USA. Stripe, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, Stripe uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

9. Booking appointments

When you book an appointment, we process the following on behalf of the salon: name, email address, phone number if you choose to provide it, and the selected service, staff member and time. The salon can add notes about you to its customer records. The purpose is to carry out the appointment, including confirmation, reminder and cancellation link by email. The legal basis for the salon is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The salon decides how long the data is kept. When the contract between the salon and Kammzeit ends, we delete the salon's data.

10. Emails

We send confirmations, reminders and login links via InstantNode's own mail servers in the EU.

11. Contact

If you write to us, we process your details to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We forward questions about a booking to the salon.

12. Google Calendar (in preparation)

In future, salons will be able to connect their staff members' Google Calendars. Only after this express authorisation does Kammzeit access the connected calendar via the Google Calendar API, and exclusively to

  • query busy times so that only free slots are offered (no titles or content of other appointments are stored), and
  • add appointments booked via Kammzeit to this calendar and change or delete them there.

We store the access keys in encrypted form. The connection can be removed at any time in Kammzeit or in the Google account settings; we then delete the keys. Kammzeit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not share this data, do not sell it and do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.

13. Retention periods

  • Salon account: until you delete the account or the contract ends. We then delete the salon's data, including customer records and appointments. It disappears from our backups no later than 30 days after deletion.
  • Login links: valid for 15 minutes. Sessions: at most 30 days, ended immediately when you log out.
  • Error logs: 14 days.
  • Rate limits: only briefly in memory, never on disk.
  • Invoices and payment records: for as long as commercial and tax law requires (§ 257 HGB, § 147 AO), currently up to ten years (accounting records such as invoices eight years, books and records ten years). This concerns our accounting and the data held by Stripe.

14. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). For booking data, please contact the salon; we support it in this. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz).

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal message to kontakt@kammzeit.de is sufficient.

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

Annex 1: Technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR)

Physical access control

  • The servers are located exclusively in the data centre of Skylink Data Centers B.V. in Eygelshoven (Kerkrade), Netherlands, with the measures customary there for access control, video surveillance, power supply and fire protection.

System access control

  • Login to the dashboard only via a one-time link sent to the email address on file, valid for 15 minutes and usable only once. Kammzeit does not use passwords.
  • Login links and session identifiers are stored in the database only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Sessions expire after 30 days and end immediately on logout. The session cookie is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax.
  • Rate limiting against mass requests, for example for login and booking.
  • Server administration only by the processor via encrypted connections (SSH).

Data access control

  • Only the processor has access to servers, database and backups.
  • A salon account only sees the data of its own salon.
  • Requests that change data in the dashboard are only possible via POST, and their origin is checked (protection against cross-site request forgery). A content security policy prevents external scripts from being loaded.

Separation control

  • Tenant separation in the application: each record is assigned to exactly one salon, and every query in the dashboard is limited to the logged-in salon.
  • Each salon has its own booking page on its own subdomain.
  • The application runs as its own restricted system user that may only write to its data directory.

Transfer control

  • All connections to the website, booking pages and dashboard are encrypted with TLS.
  • Emails are sent via InstantNode's own mail servers in the EU.
  • No disclosure of data to third parties, no tracking or analytics services, no third-party content on the pages.

Input control

  • Appointments store the time of creation and their origin (online booking or entry by the salon).
  • No access logs. Error logs are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Availability control

  • Regular backups of the database on InstantNode systems in the EU, kept for at most 30 days.
  • Redundant power supply and network connectivity in the data centre. Filtering of overload attacks (DDoS) at the network edge.

Deletion control

  • When an account is deleted, all of the salon's data is deleted, including customer records, appointments, staff, services, login links and sessions. Backups rotate after at most 30 days.
  • Expired sessions are deleted automatically.

Organisational measures

  • Access only by the processor; other persons only after a written commitment to confidentiality (§ 7).
  • Review of the measures whenever the system changes significantly.
  • Defined procedure for data breaches, including notification of the salon (§ 11(3)).
